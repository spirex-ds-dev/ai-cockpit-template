# Human Report Semantic Quality

The Human Benefit Report is a decision view of a validated Task Outcome, not an
independent authority. It must say what was completed, what was not run, what
was blocked, what remains prohibited, who must decide, and the safe next step.

It must not imply that a warning-free format proves safety, enterprise control,
provider approval, test coverage outside its evidence, or avoided impact without
the corresponding source-bound fact.

## Required decision structure

Every rendered report uses the same order: task conclusion, completed work,
findings, AI Cockpit interventions, forced stops, resolved risks, avoided
impact, unresolved risks, not-run verification, forbidden claims, human
decisions, and next safe action. Empty sections say that no record is available;
they never turn absence into a pass.

Completed work is sourced from the Task Outcome summary. Risks, stops,
limitations, prohibited claims, and decisions retain their Task Outcome source.
The avoided-impact section distinguishes a recorded prevention action from an
unsupported causal claim. This makes the report usable without making it an
independent safety, approval, enterprise-control, or verification authority.
